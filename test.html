Explore Our High School Curriculum

At Biscayne Bay Preparatory Academy, students choose a diploma path built around their goals and their graduation timeline. Our fully online, self-paced curriculum follows the standard Florida course requirements for every subject and is taught by state-certified instructors. The Academy offers three diploma paths, described below: the 18-credit ACCEL diploma, the 24-credit standard diploma, and the 24-credit Honors College Preparatory diploma.

18-Credit ACCEL DiplomaAn accelerated route to the same standard diploma in 18 credits. Students complete the same core courses; the physical education and online-course requirements are waived and the number of electives is reduced so students can finish sooner.
24-Credit Standard DiplomaThe traditional Florida high school diploma. Twenty-four credits across English, mathematics, science, social studies, physical education, the arts, and electives, meeting all state graduation requirements.
24-Credit Honors College Preparatory DiplomaA college-preparatory path for university-bound students. The 24 credits are completed at the honors level and include two credits of a world language, with SAT and ACT preparation tutoring provided as a college-readiness support (not a for-credit class), aligned with Florida State University System admission requirements.
